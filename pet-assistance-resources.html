Pet Assistance Resources

If money is standing between you and your animal's care, you're not out of options. Here are established, legitimate organizations that help pet owners directly.

These are not programs run by Posh Nonprofit Animal Transport — they're independent, established national organizations that provide financial help directly to pet owners. Eligibility, funding availability, and application steps vary by organization, so visit their sites directly for current details.

Emergency & General Veterinary Care

RedRover Relief

redrover.org

Emergency veterinary funding, disaster assistance for pet owners, and support for domestic violence survivors trying to keep their pets safe.

The Pet Fund

thepetfund.com

Financial assistance for non-emergency veterinary care, up to $500 per applicant.

Brown Dog Foundation

browndogfoundation.org

Helps with both emergency veterinary costs and ongoing chronic condition treatment.

Frankie's Friends

frankiesfriends.org

Financial assistance toward veterinary care costs for pet owners facing a diagnosis they can't afford to treat.

Bow Wow Buddies Foundation

bowwowbuddies.com

Urgent veterinary care assistance for dog owners facing a financial emergency (dogs only).

Transport-Specific

RACE4RAN

facebook.com/RACE4Ran

Provides transport services for displaced animals, plus financial assistance for related medical needs.

Pet Food & Basic Needs

Feeding Pets of the Homeless

petsofthehomeless.org

Pet food pantry locator plus veterinary care resources, focused on pet owners experiencing homelessness or housing insecurity.

Posh Nonprofit Animal Transport does not control eligibility, funding, or application decisions for any organization listed above and cannot guarantee assistance will be available. These are provided as a starting point only — please confirm current details directly with each organization.
