Our Mission

Closing the Gap Between Overcrowded and Ready

Posh Nonprofit Animal Transport moves rescue and shelter animals between overcrowded facilities and the rescues, fosters, and adopters ready to take them in – safely, calmly, and without the logistics burden falling on volunteer networks stretched thin. When disaster strikes, we deploy the same vehicle and care to move animals out of harm's way.

What We Do

Shelter & Rescue Relocation

Point-to-point transport for animals moving between shelters, rescue partners, and foster networks – including emergency and disaster-relief relocation when time is critical.

Follow the Build

A Vehicle Built for the Mission

Our transport vehicle is a fully converted Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, purpose-built for the realities of animal relocation – crash-tested kennel systems, climate control, and a self-contained power system that keeps everything running without depending on outside electricity.

That off-grid capability matters most in the moments it's hardest to plan for. During floods, wildfires, and other disasters, power and infrastructure are often the first things to fail, and that's exactly when animals need to move fastest. A vehicle that can run independently for days means we can respond when other options can't.

Every mile this van runs is funded by a combination of donors, grants, and support from our sister organization, Posh Concierge Animal Transport LLC, which rents the vehicle at fair market rate to help sustain our mission year-round.

Two Organizations, One Mission

Posh Nonprofit Animal Transport

A Missouri nonprofit corporation (501(c)(3) status pending) dedicated to rescue and shelter animal relocation and disaster-relief transport. Funded by donors, grants, and rental income from our sister LLC.

Posh Concierge Animal Transport, LLC

A premium door-to-door pet transport business serving private clients and military families alike, including PCS relocations. Revenue from the LLC helps sustain the nonprofit's mission through a fair-market vehicle rental arrangement.
